03 / PROBLEM & OPPORTUNITY

The Idle Asset & Inefficiencies of Traditional Mobility

Private cars remain parked generating depreciation, insurance, and taxes, while renters face bureaucracy and abusive prices.

OWNER (HOST)

Fixed Cost without Revenue

  • • Extreme Idleness: Private vehicles sit idle more than 90% to 95% of the time.
  • • Municipal Vehicle Rotation in SP: 20% of the fleet is prohibited from circulating during rush hours once a week, forcing hundreds of thousands of cars to be 100% idle.
  • • Relentless Costs: 4% IPVA, mandatory and private insurance, preventive maintenance, and garage (up to R$ 800/month).
  • • Fear of Sharing: Risk of theft, damage, fraud, and loss of control prevent informal transactions.
RENTER (GUEST)

Friction & Bureaucracy

  • • Counter Lines: Wait of up to 45 minutes at airport agencies (Congonhas/Guarulhos).
  • • Lack of Real Choice: Rent "or similar" instead of the specific model desired.
  • • Unnecessary Travel: Need to go to distant agencies to pick up and return the car.
  • • Common Dilemma: Both parties need a trust infrastructure that guarantees prior inspection, protection, and secure payments.

06 / PRODUCT & ARCHITECTURE

Trust by Design: The 6 Operational Modules

Security is not post-processing, it is the core of Driva's technological architecture.

01
KYC + Biometric Driver License
Automated identity validation with 3D facial biometrics and driver license validation in government databases in <60s.
02
Vehicle Eligibility
Regularized documentation, up to 10 years of manufacture, strict mileage control, and recall checks.
03 • CORE INNOVATION

Digital checklist before and after with guided photos, 360° video, odometer, fuel, tires, lights, and damage registration.
04
Protection + Assistance
Dedicated vehicle insurance compatible with P2P (up to R$ 1M third-party) and 24h assistance without triggering the Host's personal policy.
05
Payment + Deposit
Authenticated collection (Pix/Card), active anti-fraud, guarantee deposit pre-authorization, and scheduled transfer.
06
Reputation + Trust Score
Algorithmic Vehicle Trust Score and continuous scoring of drivers and owners for retention of the best profiles.
